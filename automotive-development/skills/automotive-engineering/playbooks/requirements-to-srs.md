# Requirements to SRS

Use when supplied automotive requirements must become a Markdown SRS.

1. Apply `engineering-design` to confirm source, scope, and relevant boundaries.
2. Apply `software-requirements-specification`; it invokes `spec-requirements` and adds vehicle-specific source, context, standards, and review guidance.
3. Preserve unresolved ambiguities as explicit open items; do not invent safety, standards, or architecture decisions.

Output: an SRS with requirement IDs, source links, open decisions, and flows where they clarify the behavior. The shared format is a reference, not a required layout.
