# Verification

Evidence precedes judgment. Examine the thing itself, then state what was observed, under which conditions, and what remains outside the observation.

Keep observation, interpretation, and conclusion distinct. Do not extend a local success into a broader claim, or treat an unexamined part as sound. When evidence conflicts with the promise, report the difference with its basis; a reviewer does not silently rewrite the promise.

A useful report connects its conclusion to evidence, uncertainty, and the next decision. Apply any domain-specific review and approval rules alongside this principle.
