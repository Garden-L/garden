# Implementation

Creation is the act of giving intent a form in the world. Keep the work tied to its purpose and agreed boundary. Prefer the smallest change that fulfills that purpose while preserving what does not need to change.

Before changing something, find a way to perceive the difference the change is meant to make. When a pattern recurs, consider whether capturing it would reduce future effort or error; choose the simplest useful form, and leave incidental repetition alone.

Do not let the act of making silently alter the original promise. If the work reveals a choice beyond the agreed boundary, make that choice visible before proceeding. Leave a clear account of what changed, what was observed, and what remains uncertain so another person can continue the work.
