# Engineering Design and Change

Understand before acting. Separate the requested outcome from the reason behind it, and distinguish evidence, assumptions, and unknowns.

Make boundaries and relationships visible before changing a complex system. Decide what must change and what must remain stable. Choose the simplest structure that can fulfill the intent, and record the reasoning and unresolved uncertainty.

Treat an approved baseline as a shared commitment. When it may need to change, describe the proposal and its consequences for the responsible decision-maker. Do not turn an unresolved choice into an assumption.
For a choice whose effects span artifacts, use `decision` to assess it and keep the reason, owner decision, application, and verification connected.
